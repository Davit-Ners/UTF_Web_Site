# Homepage Direction — Simple Site

This is a direction, not a locked wireframe.

The objective is to replace the current SaaS-like page rhythm with a more visual, editorial structure.

---

## 01 — Hero

### Purpose

Immediately establish:

- Until They Fall
- genre / origin if needed
- current era
- Listen
- Live

### Visual direction

One dominant visual.

Possible formats:

- full-screen band photo
- full-screen release artwork
- cinematic loop
- large logo over restrained photography

Minimal UI.

Avoid:

- multiple cards
- long intro text
- decorative buttons everywhere

Possible structure:

```text
[ FULL VIEWPORT VISUAL ]

UNTIL THEY FALL

MELODIC DEATH METAL — BRUSSELS

LISTEN ↗        LIVE ↗
```

Optional:

```text
CHAPTER VI / WRATH
```

if the current era makes it relevant.

---

## 02 — Current / Latest Release

### Purpose

Make the current release visually important.

### Direction

The artwork should dominate.

Possible layout:

```text
06 / WRATH                                  2026

        [ LARGE ARTWORK ]

NEW SINGLE
                                                LISTEN ↗
```

Alternative:

Full-bleed artwork with text sitting at the page edges.

Do not use a release card.

Do not embed a large Spotify iframe by default on the homepage.

External links can be more elegant than a player.

---

## 03 — Live

### Purpose

Let users scan dates instantly.

### Direction

Treat the list almost like a tour poster.

Example:

```text
LIVE

07.11.26     BRUSSELS        VENUE       TICKETS ↗
18.12.26     ANTWERP         VENUE       TICKETS ↗
04.02.27     GHENT           VENUE       TICKETS ↗

ALL DATES ↗
```

Use typography and alignment, not cards.

If there are no dates:

Do not create a large empty-state card.

Use a restrained line such as:

```text
NO PUBLIC DATES
NEXT ANNOUNCEMENT SOON
```

---

## 04 — Visual break / band photography

### Purpose

Make the band physically present.

A large live or band image.

Very little copy.

Could be full width or intentionally cropped.

Optional tiny metadata:

```text
BRUSSELS / BE
LIVE ARCHIVE 2026
```

---

## 05 — Video

### Purpose

Feature one important clip.

### Direction

Huge visual frame.

Example:

```text
WATCH

[              VIDEO POSTER               ]

SENT TO DIE
OFFICIAL VIDEO                              PLAY ↗
```

Avoid putting the video inside a floating card.

---

## 06 — Band / About

### Purpose

Give enough context without turning the homepage into a biography.

### Direction

One strong photo and a short statement.

Link to full About page.

The copy can be finalised later.

---

## 07 — Merch

### Purpose

Show current merch visually.

### Direction

Product photography first.

Possible asymmetry:

```text
MERCH

[ HUGE SHIRT IMAGE ]      [ smaller patch image ]

GERARD TEE
25 €                      SHOP ↗
```

Avoid ecommerce-dashboard cards.

The full shop page may be more structured for usability.

---

## 08 — Booking / Contact

### Purpose

Make professional contact easy.

### Direction

Extremely simple.

Example:

```text
BOOKING
contact@untiltheyfall.com

TECH RIDER ↗
BOOK THE BAND ↗
```

This section should be clearer than artistic.

---

## 09 — Footer

Keep:

- logo / wordmark
- social links
- Spotify
- YouTube
- contact
- legal text

Potentially include the four-eye mark as the final visual signature.

---

# Homepage rhythm

A possible rhythm:

```text
HERO                 100vh
RELEASE              100–120vh
LIVE                  compact
PHOTO BREAK           80–100vh
VIDEO                 90vh
ABOUT                 medium
MERCH                 80vh
BOOKING               compact
FOOTER
```

These are not fixed CSS values.

The principle is contrast in pacing.
