# Visual Language

## Core visual idea

**Industrial cathedral meets metal editorial design.**

The visual system should combine:

- monumental architecture
- dark religious composition
- modern editorial layout
- live photography
- technical documentation
- controlled dystopian imagery

The result should feel physical and authored.

---

## Core palette

Do not lock exact values before testing with real assets.

Start with:

### Base

- near-black
- charcoal
- dirty white / bone
- neutral grey

### Accent

Use one main accent at a time.

The accent may eventually change depending on the current Seven Sins chapter.

For example:

- Wrath → burnt red / ember
- Lust → deep wine / bruised magenta
- Gluttony → sickly warm amber / organic red-brown

Avoid rainbow systems on the same page.

---

## Texture

Possible textures:

- film grain
- photocopy noise
- paper
- dust
- scratched print
- oxidised metal
- concrete
- low-opacity scan artefacts

Texture should usually be subtle.

Do not place a texture layer merely because "metal site = grunge".

---

## Photography

Preferred characteristics:

- live movement
- imperfect framing
- flash
- silhouettes
- smoke
- backlight
- strong contrast
- close crops
- black and white when appropriate

Photography should feel documentary, not corporate.

---

## Artwork

Artwork can be shown:

- full bleed
- at very large scale
- partially cropped
- isolated against empty space
- with technical annotations
- as part of a poster-like composition

Do not reduce artwork to a small square next to a paragraph.

---

## Typography system

Target three roles:

### Display

For:

- UNTIL THEY FALL
- release titles
- section names
- chapter names
- large numbers

Characteristics:

- strong
- condensed or architectural
- high-impact
- works at huge sizes

### Editorial / body

For:

- descriptions
- about text
- practical information

Characteristics:

- highly readable
- neutral enough not to compete

### Technical / mono

For:

- dates
- coordinates
- serials
- chapter IDs
- Tower Seven references
- metadata

Use sparingly.

---

## Layout language

Prefer:

- 12-column grid as an invisible framework
- deliberate broken alignment
- overlapping media and text where readable
- content aligned to edges
- large negative space
- occasional full-viewport moments

Avoid:

- everything centered
- everything inside `max-width: 1200px` boxes
- equal-width cards
- predictable alternating left/right sections

---

## Graphic motifs

Potential recurring motifs:

- four eyes
- `TOWER 07`
- chapter numbering
- thin vertical/horizontal rules
- warning bars
- scan marks
- subtle crosshair/registration marks
- classified stamps
- system timestamps

Use only a few at once.

---

## Example atmosphere keywords

Use these to evaluate references:

- oppressive
- monumental
- cinematic
- ritual
- mechanical
- scorched
- surveilled
- human
- decayed
- deliberate
- melancholic
- violent
- restrained

Do not use these as a prompt blindly.
Each final design decision still needs a visual reason.
